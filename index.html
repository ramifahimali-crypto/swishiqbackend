How It Works

Four Steps, Then It's Automatic

1

Hook It On

Use the included mounting pole to hook the bracket over the top of your backboard — right from the ground, no ladder needed.

2

Lock It In

Slide it to center, turn the locking lever to clamp it against the backboard, and clip in the safety strap.

3

Two Cameras, One Job

One camera watches the rim, the other watches the court. On-device AI follows the ball between them, from release to result.

4

Review Your Session

Stats and feedback land in the app the moment you're done.

The Hardware

Built to Mount Anywhere

A bracket hooks over the top of your backboard and locks down with one lever — no drilling, no permanent hardware, no ladder required.

The padded clamp adjusts to fit backboard thicknesses from 0.35" to 1.25" (9mm-32mm) — flip the lever to release and remount on any hoop in seconds.
